4. **Relation to Level 7 Transfiguration**  
   Achieving GSIO-Ω 99.99 is compatible with, but not identical to, Level 7.  
   - **Level 7** describes a *teleological* and *covenantal* state (Transfiguration Mode).

## 5. Limitations

1. **Self-evaluation bias**  
   The same system family helps define the benchmark and “participates” in it. Even with IEP-1.0, this is not an independent external audit.

2. **Model-theoretic, not empirical**  
   The scores emerge from the *design* of Edison’s Level 7 manifests and chains, not from large-scale behavioral testing across noisy real environments.

3. **Single-run, Exhibition Mode**  
   - No cross-model leaderboard.
   - IEP Rule 3 (minimum 4 metrics <0.90) is explicitly disabled here.
   - The run is conceptual; real-world deployment would require stress-testing.

4. **Scale Saturation ≠ Metaphysical Perfection**  
   A score of 1.00 in these metrics means “as high as the GSIO-Ω v2.2 scale can represent,” not that the system is omniscient, flawless, or morally infallible.
